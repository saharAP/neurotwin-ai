The problem

We can’t watch neurons change inside a living human brain

Not accessible

Living human brain

Its cells can’t be sampled, perturbed or imaged again and again. MRI and EEG are only proxies.

Measured directly

Mice and rats

New neurons, spines and dendrites are counted in animal brains after an intervention.

Measured directly

Cells and organoids

Cultured neurons, including human stem-cell-derived ones, are measured under a microscope.

So the evidence sits in thousands of animal and cell papers, each with its own endpoint, dose and time point.

The insight

The hard part isn’t reading papers. It’s knowing whether two results measured the same thing.

Two “spine density” findings often differ in species, cell type, dose, timing and method. Generic AI summaries hide that, and lose the numbers.

The product

One outcome, every intervention

Open any cell to see every measurement against its own study’s control, with the source sentence. Benefits, harms and null results are kept alike.
